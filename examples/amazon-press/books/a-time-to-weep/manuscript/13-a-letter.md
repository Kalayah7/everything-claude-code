# A Letter to the One Who Is Missing Someone

Dear friend,

If you have made it to the end of this book, you have done something hard. Reading anything in grief takes effort.

This book does not end with you feeling better. That was never its aim. It hopes only that you feel a little less alone.

Here is what it hopes you will carry:

This is a time to weep. There is no schedule for it.

God is near the brokenhearted, even when you cannot feel it.

You are allowed to be honest, like Job and like Naomi. The Bible sides with the honest mourner.

Grief for a complicated love is still grief.

Comfort that does not comfort is not your failure.

Your body is grieving too. Be gentle with it.

Jesus called Mary by her name while she was weeping. He knows yours.

You can grieve with hope. Hope does not hurry grief; it holds it.

And one day, every tear will be wiped away, by God himself.

{{verse: Psalm 147:3}}

Until then, may you be held.
