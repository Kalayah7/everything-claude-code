## If you are struggling

This book is a companion for faith and reflection. It is not counseling or medical care. If your grief is making it hard to eat, sleep or get through the day, please talk to a doctor, a grief counselor or a pastor you trust. If you are in the United States and in crisis, or having thoughts of ending your life, call or text 988 at any time to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Elsewhere, please contact your local emergency number or crisis line.
