# A Guide for Grief Groups

This guide is for churches and friends who want to read *A Time to Weep* together over five gatherings, two chapters at a time. Each gathering takes about an hour.

A note for leaders: people in a grief group are carrying heavy loads. Begin each gathering by letting people say the name of the person they are grieving, if they want to. Never rush anyone, never correct anyone's feelings, and never offer explanations for a death. Allow silence. If someone mentions thoughts of self-harm, or seems unable to function, follow up privately and help them reach a doctor, counselor or pastor. In the United States, make sure they know about 988, and elsewhere, a local crisis line.

## Gathering 1: A Time to Weep and Near to the Brokenhearted (chapters 1 and 2)

- **Read together:** Ecclesiastes 3:1-8 and Psalm 34:17-19.
- **Share:** Who are you grieving? What is one thing you miss about them?
- **Discuss:** What pressure have you felt to be "past it"? Does God feel near or far right now?

## Gathering 2: Job's Friends and David (chapters 3 and 4)

- **Read together:** Job 2:11-13 and 2 Samuel 18:31-33.
- **Share:** Who has comforted you just by being there?
- **Discuss:** What things have people said that hurt? Is your grief tangled with anything complicated?

## Gathering 3: Naomi and Rachel (chapters 5 and 6)

- **Read together:** Ruth 1:15-21 and Jeremiah 31:15.
- **Share:** If you gave yourself a new name for this season, what would it be?
- **Discuss:** Is it all right to be angry with God? What does it mean that God hears weeping that refuses to be comforted?

## Gathering 4: The Body and Mary at the Tomb (chapters 7 and 8)

- **Read together:** Psalm 6:6-9 and John 20:11-18.
- **Share:** What "firsts" have been hardest, or are coming up?
- **Discuss:** What do you make of Jesus calling Mary by her name?

## Gathering 5: Grieving With Hope and Every Tear (chapters 9 and 10)

- **Read together:** 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18 and Revelation 21:1-5.
- **Share:** What has helped you most in these weeks?
- **Discuss:** What is the difference between grieving with hope and being told not to grieve?
- **Close:** Say the names of those you are grieving aloud together, and pray for one another.
