## BRIEF: a-time-to-weep

- **Reader:** a Christian adult in the first weeks or months after the death of someone they love
  (a parent, spouse, child, sibling, friend). Often given the book by a friend or a church. Tired,
  foggy, unable to concentrate for long.
- **Promise:** you will feel accompanied rather than corrected; your grief is allowed, has no
  timetable, and is met by God; you will find a few gentle ways through the hardest days.
- **Genre and format:** Christian nonfiction (grief / Christian living). eBook + 6 x 9 paperback. KDP Select 90 days.
- **Target length:** 7,000 to 11,000 words (revised after draft 1: complete at about 7,500). Grieving readers cannot concentrate long: short chapters
  (600 to 900 words), one gentle question per chapter instead of two.
- **Competitors (scan-2026-11-24.md, rank 3):** steady demand for Christian grief books and gifts;
  common reader complaints about grief books are clichés, rushing toward "healing", and length.
- **Our angle:** grief in Scripture told plainly (Job's friends, David and Absalom, Naomi, Rachel,
  Mary Magdalene); 1 Thessalonians 4:13 read correctly (grieve, but with hope); no timelines; a
  page of "things people say" that helps friends too; sympathy-gift friendly.
- **Must avoid:** "everything happens for a reason", "God needed another angel", "they're in a better
  place" as comfort offered to the reader, any timeline, any claim about where a particular person is
  now, judgment about how anyone died, medical advice. No silver linings.
- **Must include:** care note with 988 and local crisis lines; a note on when grief needs a doctor or
  counselor; gentleness about losses by suicide, pregnancy loss and complicated relationships.
- **Keywords:** christian grief book; grieving the loss of a loved one christian; sympathy gift
  christian; bible comfort for grief; loss of a parent christian book; grief small group book; first year of grief.
- **Categories:** Christian Living > Death & Grief; Christian Living > Spiritual Growth; Self-Help > Death, Grief & Bereavement.
- **Price:** eBook $3.99; paperback $8.99.
- **Series:** Middle Seasons, Book 3.
- **Sensitive:** yes, the most of any book so far. The editor applies maximum care.
