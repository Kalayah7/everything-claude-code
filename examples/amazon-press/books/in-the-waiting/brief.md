## BRIEF: in-the-waiting

- **Reader:** a Christian adult who has been praying for something for a long time (a job, a
  child, a spouse, healing, a prodigal, a door to open) and is tired. They buy late at night, after
  searching "waiting on God" or "unanswered prayer", or a friend buys it for them.
- **Promise:** you will finish this book feeling less alone and less ashamed of how hard waiting
  is, with a handful of honest, practical ways to live and pray in the middle, without being
  promised the outcome you want.
- **Genre and format:** Christian nonfiction (Christian living / prayer). Kindle eBook and 6 x 9 paperback.
  KDP Select: yes for the first 90 days (Kindle Unlimited readers sample new indie authors).
- **Target length:** 12,000 to 16,000 words. Short on purpose: competitors' reviews complain about repetition.
  (Revised after draft 1: the book is complete at about 13,000 words; adding length would mean padding.)
- **Competitors:** *Waiting on God* (indie, $2.99, ~975 reviews; one reader found it brief);
  *God Has a Waiting Room*, *Still Waiting* (praised for honesty, criticised as repetitive / dragging).
- **Our angle:** one Bible story or psalm per chapter, told in its real setting; no clichés; no
  promise that the answer is "yes"; every chapter ends with two questions and a short prayer; a group
  discussion guide at the back. Companion to the *In the Waiting* 30-day prayer journal.
- **Must include:** honest lament (the "how long" psalms); people who waited and did not receive
  (Hebrews 11); what to do with ordinary days; waiting with others.
- **Must avoid:** "God's timing is perfect" as a slogan; blaming the reader's faith; promises of
  jobs, babies, healing or spouses; health or financial advice; disputed doctrine.
- **Keyword phrases (7):** waiting on God; unanswered prayer; Christian book for hard times;
  trusting God when life is hard; how long O Lord; encouragement for waiting season; Christian
  living short book. Founder to confirm against Amazon search suggestions.
- **Categories:** Christian Living > Spiritual Growth; Christian Living > Personal Growth / Inspirational;
  Prayer. (Pick the closest current KDP categories at upload.)
- **Price:** eBook $3.99 (inside the 70% royalty band, a little above the $2.99 indie anchor);
  paperback $9.99 (set after KDP shows the printing cost).
- **Series plan:** *Middle Seasons* books: 1 In the Waiting, 2 Be Still (anxiety), 3 Held (grief),
  each paired with the studio's journal.
- **Sensitive:** yes. Infertility, illness and loss will be on readers' minds. No timelines, no
  "everything happens for a reason", point to pastors, counselors and doctors where fitting.
- **Scripture:** World English Bible (public domain), looked up at build time from references, never typed.
