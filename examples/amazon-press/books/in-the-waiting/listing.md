<p><b>For everyone who has prayed for something for a long time, and is still waiting.</b></p>
<p>Maybe it is a job, a diagnosis, a child, a marriage, a way out. You asked God a long time ago, and the answer has not come. You are in the long middle: the stretch between asking and knowing.</p>
<p><i>In the Waiting</i> is a short, honest book for that place. It will not promise you that what you want is on its way. <li><b>Paul and the faithful of Hebrews 11</b>, who did not receive what they asked for and were held anyway</li>

<p>Each of the twelve short chapters ends with two honest questions and a brief prayer. A six-week discussion guide at the back makes it easy to read with a friend or a small group.</p>
<p>Gentle, practical and free of clichés, <i>In the Waiting</i> is for anyone in a season of unanswered prayer, and a thoughtful gift for a friend who is.</p>
