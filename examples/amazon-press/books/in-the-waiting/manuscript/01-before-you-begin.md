# Before You Begin

If you picked up this book, I suspect you are waiting for something.

Maybe it is a job. Maybe it is a diagnosis, or a cure. Maybe it is a child you have prayed for, or a child you have prayed *about* for years. Maybe it is a husband or a wife, a home, a way out of debt, a phone call from someone who stopped calling. Maybe you are not even sure anymore what you are waiting for. You only know that you asked God for something a long time ago, and the answer has not come.

This is a short book about that place. I call it the long middle: the stretch between asking and knowing.

Before you read any further, I want to tell you what this book will not do.

It will not promise you that the thing you want is on its way. I do not know that, and anyone who tells you they know it is guessing. It will not tell you that you are still waiting because your faith is too small, or because there is a hidden sin you need to find, or because God is teaching you a lesson you keep failing to learn. It will not hand you a slogan to put on the fridge.

What it will do is sit with you in the middle, and look at what the Bible actually says about people who waited. There are a great many of them. Some waited decades. Some complained loudly. Some tried to hurry God along and made a mess. Some got exactly what they asked for. Some did not, and died still trusting. Their stories are in Scripture, I think, partly so that people like us would know we are not the first.

Each chapter is short and focuses on one story or one idea. At the end of each one, you will find a section called *For the waiting room*, with two questions and a short prayer. You can write your answers in a notebook, think them over on a walk, or skip them altogether. There is a guide at the back if you would like to read this book with a friend or a small group.

A few practical notes. Bible quotations come from the World English Bible, a modern translation in the public domain. Where it uses the name Yahweh, many other translations print "the LORD". If your waiting involves your health, your mind, your safety or your money, please do not let any book, including this one, replace a doctor, a counselor, a pastor or a trusted advisor. Praying and getting help go together.

And one more thing. You do not have to read this book quickly, or in one go, or with a cheerful heart. You are allowed to read it tired. Most people who need it will.

Let's begin in the middle, since that is where you are.
