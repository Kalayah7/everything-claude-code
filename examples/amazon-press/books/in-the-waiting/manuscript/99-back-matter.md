# A Note Before You Go

If this book helped you, even a little, would you consider leaving an honest review on Amazon? A sentence or two is plenty. Reviews help other people who are waiting find a book that might keep them company.

## Keep going

If you would like to keep praying through your season, *In the Waiting* also exists as a 30-day guided prayer journal: one verse, a short reflection, two questions and room to write, every day.

## If you are struggling

This book is a companion for faith and reflection. It is not counseling or medical care. If you are finding it hard to cope, please talk to a doctor, a licensed counselor or a pastor you trust. If you are in the United States and in crisis, you can call or text 988 at any time to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Elsewhere, please contact your local emergency number or crisis line.
