# Outline: In the Waiting

**Promise:** you are not alone, waiting is not a failure of faith, and there are honest ways to
live and pray in the middle, without anyone promising you the ending.
**Reader:** a tired Christian who has prayed a long time for something that has not come.
**Shape:** 12 short chapters (1,000 to 1,400 words). Each opens in a real Bible scene or a
plainly labelled illustration, makes one point, and closes with "For the waiting room": two
questions and a short prayer. Discussion guide at the back.
