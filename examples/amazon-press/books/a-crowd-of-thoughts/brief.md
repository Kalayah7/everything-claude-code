## BRIEF: a-crowd-of-thoughts

- **Reader:** a Christian adult whose mind races: worry, overthinking, a tight chest at night. Often
  ashamed, because they have been told that anxiety means weak faith. Buys after searching
  "Christian book for anxiety" or "Bible verses for anxiety", or is given it by a friend.
- **Promise:** you will finish this book less ashamed of your anxiety, with a handful of honest
  ways to bring a racing mind to God, and with permission to get real help as well.
- **Genre and format:** Christian nonfiction (Christian living / emotions). eBook + 6 x 9 paperback. KDP Select for 90 days.
- **Target length:** 10,000 to 14,000 words (revised after draft 1: complete at about 10,500; padding would hurt it).
- **Competitors (scan-2026-11-24.md):** *A Small Book for the Anxious Heart* (50 short meditations,
  praised for depth), *A Verse a Day for the Anxious Soul*, *5-Minute Devotions for Worry*, *Anxiety
  Elephants*. Field is crowded with big publishers.
- **Our angle:** Scripture people who were openly anxious or overwhelmed (David, Elijah, Paul, Jesus
  in Gethsemane, the disciples in the storm), told in context; never "just trust more"; a whole
  chapter saying that doctors, counseling and medication are not a failure of faith; same shape as
  book 1 (two questions and a prayer per chapter, a group guide).
- **Must avoid:** diagnosing; medical, dosage or treatment advice; implying anxiety is sin or a
  lack of faith; promising the anxiety will go away; "Philippians 4:6 means stop feeling anxious".
- **Must include:** a clear care note up front and at the back (988 in the US); a gentle treatment
  of Elijah asking to die, with crisis help named right there.
- **Keywords:** christian book for anxiety; bible verses for anxiety; overthinking christian;
  worry and faith; anxious mind christian living; peace of god anxiety; gift for anxious friend christian.
- **Categories:** Christian Living > Spiritual Growth; Christian Living > Personal Growth; Self-Help > Anxieties & Phobias
  (only if the founder is comfortable; the book is faith-based reflection, not self-help therapy).
- **Price:** eBook $3.99; paperback $8.99 to $9.99 after printing cost.
- **Series:** Middle Seasons, Book 2 (after *In the Waiting*).
- **Sensitive:** yes, highly. The editor applies extra care.
