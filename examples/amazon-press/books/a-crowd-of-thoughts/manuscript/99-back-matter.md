## If you are struggling

This book is a companion for faith and reflection. It is not counseling or medical care, and it does not diagnose or treat anxiety. If anxiety is affecting your sleep, eating, work or relationships, please talk to a doctor or a licensed counselor. If you are in the United States and in crisis, or having thoughts of harming yourself, call or text 988 at any time to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Elsewhere, please contact your local emergency number or crisis line.
