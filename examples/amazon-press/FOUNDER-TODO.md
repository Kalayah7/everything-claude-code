# Founder To-Do

Only you can do these. The `/press` team prepares everything else.

## One-time setup

- [ ] Create a KDP account at kdp.amazon.com and complete the tax interview and bank details.
- [ ] Choose an imprint name and pen name. Search Amazon and the USPTO trademark search to make
      sure neither belongs to someone else. Add them to `press.config.json`.
- [ ] Read the current KDP Content Guidelines and the AI-generated content rules.
- [ ] Covers: `node scripts/cover.js <slug>` makes a simple typographic eBook cover (JPG) and a
      paperback full wrap (PDF) sized from the page count. Use them, or hire a designer for stronger ones.
- [ ] Set your monthly goal and weekly hours in `press.config.json`.

## Each book (about 60 to 90 minutes)

- [ ] Read the editor's PASS report and skim the manuscript. You approve it, not the agents.
- [ ] Upload the eBook: title, subtitle, series, description, keywords and categories from `listing.md`.
- [ ] Answer the AI-generated content question honestly: **yes**.
- [ ] Choose KDP Select (Kindle Unlimited, eBook exclusive to Amazon for 90 days) or not, as the brief recommends.
- [ ] Set the price, preview the book in Kindle Previewer or the online previewer, and publish.
- [ ] Repeat for the paperback (free KDP ISBN or your own), check the printed proof if you ordered one.
- [ ] Tell the managing director the publish date, and later the royalties from the KDP reports.

## Never

- Buy or swap reviews, or give anything in exchange for one.
- Use another author's name, book title or trademark in a title, subtitle or keywords.
