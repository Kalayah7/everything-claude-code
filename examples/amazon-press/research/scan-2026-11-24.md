## SCAN: 2026-11-24   Question: what should our first books be?

Scope: start where the founder's faith studio already has a voice, a reader and products to
cross-sell (guided prayer journals for hard seasons). Other genres can be scanned on request.

| Rank | Idea (working title) | Reader | Demand | Gap | Fit | Series | Risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the Waiting: Finding God in the Long Middle | Christians in a long season of unanswered prayer | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 22 |

### Evidence per idea

- **Waiting / unanswered prayer**
  - An indie title, *Waiting on God* (Cherie Hills), listed on eReaderIQ at $2.99 with about 975
    reviews: buyers pay for this topic at an indie price.
    https://www.ereaderiq.com/dp/B0059XEAG8
  - New books keep arriving in the space (*God, Why Didn't You?*, NetGalley, May 2026; *Silence*,
    Paul Bucks), so the subject is current. https://www.netgalley.com/catalog/book/859543
  - Gap, from reader reviews of similar books: "a bit repetitive" (*God Has a Waiting Room*),
    "became very repetitive" (NetGalley review), "repetitive at times ... seemed to drag"
    (*Still Waiting*, Goodreads). One reader found a *Waiting on God* edition "good but brief".
    Our angle: short, never padded, honest, and with a real study guide.
- **Anxiety**: many short devotionals sell (*A Small Book for the Anxious Heart*, *A Verse a Day for
  the Anxious Soul*, *5-Minute Devotions for Worry*; *Worry Less, Pray More* shows 4.7 stars from
  424 reviews on Walmart). The field is crowded with big publishers, and the topic needs extra care around medical claims.
  https://www.crosswalk.com/culture/books/christian-books-on-anxiety.html
- **Grief**: strong steady demand but the most sensitive subject; better as book 3 after the voice is proven.

### Recommendation

- Write first: **In the Waiting**. Evidence of paying readers at indie prices, a clear complaint we
  can beat (repetition), a matching journal already built, and a natural series (Be Still, Held, Begin Again).
- What I could not check: live Amazon Best Sellers Rank, Amazon search-bar suggestions, and Amazon
  reviews (no direct Amazon pages opened in this scan). **Founder: type "waiting on God" and
  "unanswered prayer" into the Amazon search bar and note the suggestions before publishing.**
