# Launch Content (first 2 weeks)

Drafts for the founder's own LinkedIn or Facebook and local business groups. Post at most one a
day. Help in group threads before posting your own. These posts teach. They do not hard-sell.

## Post 1: the hook

> I spent this week reading Google reviews for local businesses in [city].
>
> The surprising part: most of the businesses I checked had great reviews that the owner never
> replied to. Some had complaints sitting unanswered for months.
>
> A reply takes 2 minutes and is the first thing a new customer reads after the stars.
>
> I'm starting [Company] to handle this for local owners. If you run a business here and want
> a free look at your reviews, comment or DM me and I'll send you ready-to-post replies.

(Only use "most of the businesses I checked" if the research sample really shows that. Otherwise
use the real number.)

## Post 2: how to reply to a 1-star review

> How to reply to a 1-star review without making it worse:
>
> 1. Thank them, even if it stings.
> 2. Own the part that's fair ("you should have gotten a call when we were late").
> 3. Don't argue facts in public.
> 4. Give a direct way to fix it: a phone number or an email.
> 5. Sign with your name.
>
> Future customers aren't judging the complaint. They're judging how you respond.

## Post 3: the thank-you reply

> Most owners skip replying to 5-star reviews. That's a missed chance.
>
> A specific thank-you ("so glad the same-day fix saved your weekend, Sarah!") tells the next
> customer you actually remember people. Takes 30 seconds.

## Post 4: asking for reviews the right way

> The legal, Google-approved way to get more reviews:
>
> - Ask every customer, not just the happy ones. Asking only happy customers ("review gating")
>   breaks Google's rules.
> - Never offer a discount or gift for a review.
> - Make it easy: a QR code at the counter, plus the link in your receipt or follow-up text.
>
> Buying or faking reviews can get your profile suspended. It's also illegal under FTC rules.

## Post 5: soft offer (after 1 real client result, with permission)

> [Client name] had [N] unanswered reviews. In [weeks], every one got a reply, and they've
> replied to every new review within 24 hours since.
>
> If you'd like the same for your business, it's $[price]/month, cancel anytime: [site URL]

Use only real, permitted numbers. Skip this post until you have them.

## Group etiquette

- Read each group's rules. Many ban self-promotion except on certain days.
- Answer questions about reviews and Google profiles generously. Credibility creates the DMs.
- Never DM members who did not ask.
