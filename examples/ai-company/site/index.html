Questions

Do you use AI?

Yes. Replies are drafted with AI using your tone and the details of each review. Then they are checked against your guidelines before posting. Replies to negative reviews always wait for your approval.

Will you write reviews for my business?

No, never. Fake reviews are illegal and against Google's rules. We reply to real reviews and give you honest, compliant ways to ask your real customers for feedback.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. You can remove us in one click at any time.

What if I don't like a reply?

Tell us and we will edit or remove it the same day. We will also update your guidelines so it does not happen again.

Can you guarantee more stars or a higher ranking?

No one can honestly guarantee that. What we guarantee is the work: every review answered within 24 hours and four profile posts every month.
