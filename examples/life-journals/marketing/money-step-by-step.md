## Description, version 1 (to the reader)

Quick question. Did anyone ever sit you down and explain how money works?

How a paycheck shrinks before it reaches you. What APR means. Which student loan grows while
you are still in class. What to do with a first credit card. How people get out of debt.

If the answer is no, you are in the same place as nearly everyone else. Most of us were never
taught. We found out by making mistakes, and some of those were expensive.

This journal is the lesson you should have had. You get it in small pieces, at the point in
your life when you need it.

**Start at the step you are on:**

- **Step 1, High school.** Needs and wants, your first paycheck, a bank account, how money grows, saving for a goal, what borrowing costs, paying for what comes next.
- **Step 2, College.** Making aid money last a whole term, knowing your loans, a first credit card, small leaks, rent and roommates, work and study, scams.
- **Step 3, Just graduated.** Reading a job offer, a real-life budget, when loan payments start, an emergency fund, your employer's retirement match, insurance, pay rises.
- **Step 4, In debt.** Writing it all down, what interest really costs, stopping the leak, choosing a payoff order, calling lenders, your credit report, life after the last payment.

Each lesson is one page: a short reading, one key word explained in plain English, two
questions to answer about your own life, and one thing to do today. There are 28 lessons, so
by the end you will know 28 money words that many adults cannot explain.

**"Why a journal and not a video?"**
Because you forget what you watch. Researchers who pulled together 126 studies found that
teaching money skills has a small but real effect on what people do, and that it works best at
a "teachable moment", when you can use the lesson straight away. So each page asks you to
write your own numbers and take one real action today.

**"I am in debt already. Is it too late for me?"**
No. Go straight to Step 4. It stands on its own. On the first day you make the full list of
what you owe. Most people skip that step, and nothing else works until it is done.

**"Will this make me rich or get me out of debt?"**
No book can promise that, and you should be careful of any that does. This one teaches you how
things work and helps you make your own plan. The doing is yours.
