# Customer Reply Templates

Customer care drafts from these. The founder sends. Reply within 24 hours.

| Situation | Reply |
|---|---|
| Cannot find the download | "Thanks for your order. On Etsy, go to You > Purchases and reviews, and click Download Files next to the order. On a phone, use a browser, not the app. If it still will not open, reply here and I will sort it out." |
| Wants a refund (digital) | "I'm sorry it was not what you hoped for. Digital files cannot be returned, but I do not want you to be out of pocket for something you cannot use. I have refunded your order." (Founder issues the refund. Default to yes for a first request under $20.) |
| Wants another Bible translation | "Thank you for asking. We use the World English Bible because it is free for anyone to print. Other translations have printing limits we have to respect. You are welcome to write your preferred wording next to each verse." |
| Asks whether AI was used | "Yes. The journals are planned, written and laid out with the help of AI tools, then read and edited page by page by a person. The Scripture is quoted directly from the World English Bible." |
| Wants to print for a group | "Yes, with the group license, which covers up to 25 people: {{GROUP_LINK}}. More than 25? Tell me how many and I will set it up." |
| Found a mistake | "Thank you, you are right. I have fixed it and attached the corrected file." (Staff fixes the product and rebuilds the same day.) |
| Shares something painful | Thank them plainly. Do not give advice. If they mention harming themselves, give the 988 number (US) and urge them to contact someone they trust today. Tell the founder at once. |
