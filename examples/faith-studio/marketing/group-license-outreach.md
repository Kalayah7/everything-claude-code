# Group License Outreach

Optional lever. The marketer drafts; the founder sends from the brand address. 5 to 10 a week.

## Who

Leaders who already run a group for people in the season the journal serves: grief support and
care ministries, job-seeker groups hosted by churches, women's and men's small groups, hospital
and hospice chaplains, Christian counselors.

## Rules

- Only addresses the organization publishes for contact. Never bought, scraped in bulk or guessed.
- One short, personal email. One follow-up after 7 days. Then stop.
- Honest From name and subject. Include the brand's postal address and "reply stop and I will
  not write again". Honor it for good.
- Attach or link the free 7-day sample. Give before asking.
- Say plainly that the journal was made with AI assistance and reviewed by a person.
