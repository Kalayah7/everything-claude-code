# Email Welcome Sequence (own store)

Sent to people who buy from the own store or download the free sample. Three emails, plain
text. The founder loads these into the store's email tool once. Every email needs the brand's
postal address and an unsubscribe link, which the email tool adds.

## Free sample (the list builder)

A 7-day excerpt of the best-selling journal (days 1 to 7 and the first weekly pause page),
offered free on the store in exchange for an email address.

## Email 1: right away

Subject: Your journal is here

Hi,

Thank you for getting {{PRODUCT}}. Your download link is below.

One suggestion: pick the time of day now. Ten minutes with coffee, at lunch, or before bed. The
people who finish a journal are almost always the ones who chose a time on day one.

If anything is wrong with the file, reply to this email and a person will fix it.

{{BRAND}}

## Email 2: day 4

Subject: If you have missed a day

Hi,

If you have missed a day or three, you are not behind. There is no behind. Open to the next
page and start there.

If a question on the page feels too big, write one sentence. One sentence counts.

{{BRAND}}

## Email 3: day 12

Subject: For the people you are praying with

Hi,

Some people have asked whether they can print {{PRODUCT}} for a small group, a class or a care
ministry. Yes: the group license covers printing for up to 25 people: {{GROUP_LINK}}

And if this journal has helped, an honest review on the shop helps someone else in the same
season find it. Only if you want to.

{{BRAND}}

Rules: never offer anything in exchange for a review, never ask only happy customers, and
never write a review for anyone.
