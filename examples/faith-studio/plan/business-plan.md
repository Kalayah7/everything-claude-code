# Business Plan: Middle Seasons

## The business in one paragraph

Middle Seasons sells printable guided prayer journals for specific hard seasons: waiting,
grief, anxiety, job loss, new beginnings. Each journal is 30 days: one Bible verse, a short
reflection, two honest questions and room to pray. Buyers print at home or write on a tablet.
The AI staff researches, writes, lays out and prepares every product and listing. The founder
reads and approves each journal, uploads it, and confirms the money: about 2 hours a week.

## Why this niche

| Test | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is it you? | Faith, purpose and personal growth, with no face or name needed |
| Is it needed? | People search for help by season ("prayer journal for grief", "waiting on God"). Generic prayer journals are everywhere at $2 to $5. Season-specific ones are fewer and can charge more |
| Can AI staff do most of it? | Yes. Writing, layout, images, listing copy, pins, replies and bookkeeping are all drafted by agents |
| Is it low effort to run? | Digital files, no shipping, no inventory, no client calls |
| What makes it different? | One season per journal, honest tone, no prosperity promises, Scripture quoted exactly, group licenses for ministries |

**Assumption to confirm:** the journals are written from a broadly Christian, non-denominational
point of view. If that is not your tradition, tell the director and the voice guide changes.

## Offers

| Offer | Price | Who buys |
|---|---|---|
| Single journal, personal use | $8 | A person in that season, or a friend buying a gift |
| Seasons bundle, 3 journals | $19 | Repeat buyers, gift givers |
| Group license, print for up to 25 | $49 | Small-group leaders, care ministries, counselors, chaplains |

The group license is the most important offer for the goal. One group sale earns what six
single sales do, and ministries buy again for the next study.

## Channels

1. **Etsy**: buyers are already searching there. Requires honest AI disclosure and real human
   review of every listing. Etsy tightened its AI rules in 2026; the founder must read the
   current Creativity Standards before the first listing.
2. **Own store** (Payhip free plan to start): lower fees, bundles, group licenses, and an email
   list the business owns. This protects the business if a marketplace changes its rules.
3. **Pinterest**: free, faceless traffic to both. The staff makes 5 pins per product.
4. **Phase 2, from week 6**: paperback editions on Amazon KDP (with its AI disclosure), once a
   journal has sold and been reviewed.

## Revenue math

Run `node scripts/studio.js plan` for live numbers. With the starting assumptions:

- Average net per order: about $11.59 (75% singles, 17% bundles, 8% group licenses)
- $5,000 net needs about **432 orders a month, or 14 a day**
- At a 2% visit-to-order rate, that is about **21,600 visits a month**

## Honest odds

This is an estimate, not data from this business. Starting with no audience and 2 hours a week:

| Outcome by month 4 | What it takes |
|---|---|
| $100 to $500 a month | The likely result if the plan is followed and nothing breaks out |
| $500 to $1,500 a month | A few listings rank on Etsy or a few pins take off |
| $5,000 a month | The stretch goal. Needs a strong seasonal hit, steady group-license sales, or both |

$5,000 in month 4 is possible but unlikely on 2 hours a week. The plan is built so that every
week adds products that keep selling, and so that the numbers show early whether it is working.
More founder time on group-license outreach is the fastest lever (see below).

## Roadmap

| Weeks | Staff ships | Founder does | Should be true |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the Waiting, built and ready | One-time setup; list product 1 | Shop and store are live |
| 2-4 | 2 journals a week: new year, anxiety, grief, job loss | Weekly routine | 8 listings; first sales; first review |
| 5-8 | 2 a week; first bundle; first group-license pages; email sequence live | Weekly routine | 16 listings; 1 order a day |
| 9-12 | 2 a week; Lent journal by Jan 25; KDP paperbacks of the top 2 | Weekly routine | 24 listings; 3 to 5 orders a day |
| 13-16 | Double down on what sells; retire what does not | Weekly routine | Real numbers decide the next quarter |

Seasonal dates: new-year journals sell from Dec 26 to mid January. Lent 2027 begins Feb 10.
Mother's Day is May 9. Advent 2027 products should be listed by Oct 15.

## Levers to reach $5,000

1. **Catalog size.** Each good listing is a small, lasting source of orders. Target 24 by week 12.
2. **Group licenses.** 10 a week is about $1,900 a month. The marketer drafts short, personal
   emails to care-ministry and small-group leaders; the founder sends them. This costs about
   30 extra minutes a week and is optional.
3. **Bundles.** Raise the average order.
4. **Ads, later.** Only after 30 verified orders prove the conversion rate, and only from profit.

## Costs

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Etsy | $0.20 per listing, plus fees per sale (about 9.5% + $0.25) |
| Payhip free plan | 5% per sale plus card fees |
| Pinterest business account | Free |
| Optional domain | About $12 a year |

Starting cost is under $10. Set aside 25% of net for tax.

## Kill criteria (decided now)

The director recommends a change of niche, offer or channel if any of these happen:

- 12 listings live for 30 days with fewer than 300 total visits
- 1,000 visits with fewer than 5 orders
- More than 5% of orders refunded or disputed
- The weekly routine takes the founder more than 3 hours for 3 weeks running
- A marketplace rejects or removes listings for policy reasons twice

## Risks

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| Marketplace AI rules change | Disclose plainly, founder reviews every product, own store from day one |
| Crowded market | One season per journal, group licenses, honest tone, exact Scripture |
| A reflection is wrong or hurtful | Editor gate, founder approval, care note in every journal, fast fixes |
| Low traffic | Pinterest pins weekly, seasonal products listed early, email list |
| Copying | Personal-use license, group license, files kept out of the public repo |
